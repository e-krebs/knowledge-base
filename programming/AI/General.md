## opinions
[What are AI Agents? why do they matter?](https://addyo.substack.com/p/what-are-ai-agents-why-do-they-matter) — distinguishes agents from one-shot LLM calls by persistent context, tool use, and autonomous multi-step planning
[don't be a meat proxy](https://gruhn.me/blog/2026-08-03/) — urges reading and rewriting AI output yourself instead of just relaying it
["help" doesn't mean what it meant](https://kyleshevlin.com/help-doesnt-mean-what-it-meant/) — mourns that helping now means writing specs, not hands-on coding and learning by doing
[clanker: a word for the machine](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/5/26/clankers/) — argues cold mechanical language for AI keeps responsibility with humans, not the tool
[agentic coding is a trap](https://larsfaye.com/articles/agentic-coding-is-a-trap) — warns heavy AI orchestration erodes coding and debugging skills over time
[comprehension debt: the hidden cost of AI generated code](https://medium.com/@addyosmani/comprehension-debt-the-hidden-cost-of-ai-generated-code-285a25dac57e) — warns tests and specs alone can't substitute for genuinely understanding what shipped
[AI companies will fail](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/ng-interactive/2026/jan/18/tech-ai-bubble-burst-reverse-centaur) — frames workers as blamed 'reverse centaurs' propping up an AI growth-stock bubble
[code is clay](https://campedersen.com/code-is-clay) — argues AI will automate routine code the way factories automated pottery, leaving craft for humans
[treat agent output like compiler output](https://skiplabs.io/blog/codegen_as_compiler) — argues we still lack the specs, tests, and monitoring that made compiler trust possible
[The Multiplayer AI Manifesto](https://multiplayer-ai.com/) — argues ai agents should be shared team sessions, not private one-person chats
